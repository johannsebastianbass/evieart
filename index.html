Hello, I'm Evie

My name is Evelys, and I'm an illustrator from Brazil. I'm inspired by the simple things in life, especially nature.

I'm not afraid of getting lost in the world of fantasy, so my art is a little fanciful, always with a touch of magic. I paint digitally in Procreate.
